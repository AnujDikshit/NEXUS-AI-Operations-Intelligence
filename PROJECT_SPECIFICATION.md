# NEXUS — Project Specification

## 1. Project Name

NEXUS — AI-Powered Business Operations Intelligence Platform

## 2. Vision

NEXUS is an end-to-end business intelligence and machine learning platform that transforms operational data into actionable decisions.

## 3. Core Questions

1. What happened?
2. Why did it happen?
3. What will happen next?
4. What should we do?

## 4. Business Scenario

NEXUS simulates a retail/e-commerce business and analyzes its operational ecosystem.

The platform will work with:

- Customers
- Products
- Orders
- Payments
- Inventory
- Warehouses
- Deliveries
- Returns

## 5. Major Capabilities

- Business KPI monitoring
- Revenue and profit analysis
- Inventory intelligence
- Demand forecasting
- Delivery risk prediction
- Anomaly detection
- Root-cause analysis
- ML explainability
- Business recommendations
- AI-powered data interaction

## 7. Development Principle

Every major component will be:

1. Designed
2. Implemented
3. Tested
4. Documented
5. Integrated

No business results, model performance, or impact metrics will be fabricated.
